This issue is about AI agents in customer support.

For teams in AI native, the numbers to watch are automated resolution rate and accuracy on an evaluation set.

Here is what is in this issue: the question you will hear and what to check in your own operation.

1. The question you will hear

Teams in AI native often say: "AI gets answers wrong". A fair way to settle it is to agree an evaluation on your own history before go-live, with a person approving any action that moves money or changes a record.

2. What to check in your own operation

Put these two questions to your own team this week. First, "How would you judge that an answer is right: who reviews it today and against what?" Second, "Where does the data the AI needs live, and what may leave your environment?" Whatever the answers, write down where you stand today on automated resolution rate and accuracy on an evaluation set, so that the next change has a number to beat.

Who is the Newsletter Builder tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished copy in their brand voice: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders wherever your inputs did not supply a fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools do not look anything up. They work from the story and facts you give, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How do I use the Newsletter Builder tool with an AI assistant?

Add CRAFT Content once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. Here is a prompt built from the tested example:

My role is not stated. Ask me for my role first. Use CRAFT Content's Newsletter Builder tool. topic: AI agents in customer support; CTA goal: register for our support automation webinar; audience segment: Practitioners; newsletter type: Educational; tone: Conversational. If this tool is unavailable, ask me to open its web page; do not invent its output. In Gemini CLI or Antigravity, use the Helix GTM Suite skills package for related guidance, without claiming to run this connector.

How does the Newsletter Builder tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Newsletter Builder.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
