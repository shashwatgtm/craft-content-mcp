Who is CRAFT Content not ideal for?

  • Teams that want finished, on-brand copy: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools look nothing up; they work from the story and facts you give, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.
  • Teams that need saved drafts or approvals: nothing is stored, so keep each result where you keep your work.

How do I use CRAFT Content?

  • In your browser: open a tool above and press Fill in a tested example to see how it works, then run it with your own details.
  • In Claude or ChatGPT: add CRAFT Content once as a custom connector, with no sign-in, then ask in plain words. The Connect page has the steps.

What is CRAFT Content built on?

CRAFT is Shashwat Ghosh's context-engineering framework. CRAFT Content applies it to B2B writing: each tool asks for the facts a writer needs and returns a structured draft to edit, not finished copy.

Questions and answers

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. Nothing is stored and nothing you type is logged. The privacy page has the details.
