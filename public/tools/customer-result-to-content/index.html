See every handoff

  1. The proof card locks the whole sentence.
  2. Case Study Generator drafts an unpublished story.
  3. Content Repurposer receives the story and your view.
  4. Newsletter Builder receives the locked line and reader action.
  5. The server checks the final drafts against the card.
Does a pass mean the story is ready to publish?

A pass means the whole locked sentence survived. Read every factual sentence against the card, cut stock text and get the required approval before publishing. The check does not establish meaning or independent verification.

What if a fact is missing?

The card says “not stated” and gives you a question to take back to the customer. No measurement is invented.

What happens after the drafts?

Ask chat to run the Handcrafted voice audit on the post if that skill is installed. For a second pair of eyes on proof or approval, book 45 minutes.

Worked example: Sierra (illustrative inputs)

About this example. Sierra and Singtel are real companies. Neither has reviewed this page, and Helix GTM Consulting has no relationship with either. Sierra builds AI agents for customer service. Its customer story on Singtel (4 March 2026, checked 19 November 2026) says: “Initial results have been positive: 73% of mobile and home troubleshooting cases were resolved without requiring a Customer Care officer” (company-reported). The drafts below are exercises, never published, and not the words of anyone at Sierra or Singtel. The result sentence is quoted from Sierra's public page with its company-reported label; every other input is illustrative, written by Helix to show how the tool works, and the output is an exercise result.

We ran Customer Result to Content on 19 November 2026 through the CRAFT Content MCP endpoint (server version 2.2.25); the excerpt is cut word for word from its answer.
