The Case Against the Usual View of AI in Customer Support

By the author. Written for Support leaders at consumer apps with growing ticket backlogs.

Support automation should be measured by tickets resolved, not tickets deflected. For support leaders at consumer apps with growing ticket backlogs, the measure to watch is automated resolution rate.

The objection: AI gets answers wrong

Someone at the table will say: "AI gets answers wrong". A fair way to settle it is to agree an evaluation on your own history before go-live, with a person approving any action that moves money or changes a record.

Who has to agree

Whoever reads this will not decide alone. The owner of the workflow being automated signs; the data or AI lead evaluates the model; security and legal review data use and privacy; the operations team that hands work to the AI uses it.

What to do next

Measure it on automated resolution rate, before and after the change.

Then take this question into the next meeting: "Which cases or decisions would you trust an AI to handle first, and which never?"

Who is Thought Leadership Series not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished, on-brand copy: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools look nothing up; they work from the story and facts you give, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How do I use Thought Leadership Series from Claude?

Add CRAFT Content once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use CRAFT Content's Thought Leadership Series tool. topic: AI in customer support; your take: Support automation should be measured by tickets resolved, not tickets deflected; target reader: Support leaders at consumer apps with growing ticket backlogs; number of articles: 1; article type: Contrarian.

How does Thought Leadership Series work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Thought Leadership Series.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
