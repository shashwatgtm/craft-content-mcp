Thought Leadership Series

Generate thought leadership article drafts (about 600 to 800 words each) from your topic, your take and your proof points. Without proof points, the drafts include suggested proof points to gather. Drafts contain placeholders to complete before publishing.

Run Thought Leadership Series in your browser

Required: Topic, Your take, Target reader. Fields marked optional can be left empty.

The topic you want to establish authority on

Your unique perspective or opinion on this topic. What do you believe that others don't? What's your contrarian view?

Who should read this? Be specific (e.g., 'B2B SaaS founders struggling with churn' not just 'marketers')

OPTIONAL: Evidence supporting your take - personal stories, client examples, data/stats (comma-separated). If not provided, we'll suggest proof points to gather

Optional: Your role and why you're credible (e.g., '15 years in enterprise sales')

Number of articles to generate (1-5)

Use it from Claude or another assistant

Connect CRAFT Content once (see Connect), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use CRAFT Content's Thought Leadership Series tool. topic: Customer onboarding; your take: Onboarding is a sales problem, not a support problem; target reader: B2B SaaS founders struggling with churn; num articles: 1; article type: contrarian.

MCP tool name: thought_leadership_series. REST: POST https://craft-content.gtmhelix.com/api/tools/thought_leadership_series (see Docs).

How this tool works

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the MCP server run the same function, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
