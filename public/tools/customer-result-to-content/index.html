Does a pass mean the story is ready to publish?

A pass means the whole locked sentence survived. Read every factual sentence against the card, cut stock text and get the required approval before publishing. The check does not establish meaning or independent verification.

What if a fact is missing?

The card says “not stated” and gives you a question to take back to the customer. No measurement is invented.

What happens after the drafts?

Ask chat to run the Handcrafted voice audit on the post if that skill is installed. For a second pair of eyes on proof or approval, book 45 minutes.
