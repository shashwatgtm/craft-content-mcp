Once you accept that onboarding is a sales problem, not a support problem, a different path forward becomes clear.

First, you have to unlearn the habits that are working against you. This is harder than learning new ones. It means questioning assumptions you didn't even know you had.

Second, you need a new framework for thinking about Customer onboarding. Not a rigid system, those fail the moment reality deviates from the plan. But a set of principles that guide decision-making when the playbook doesn't apply.

Who is Thought Leadership Series not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the required inputs (Topic, Your take, Target reader); the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished, on-brand copy: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools look nothing up and use only the story and facts you give.

How does Thought Leadership Series work?
