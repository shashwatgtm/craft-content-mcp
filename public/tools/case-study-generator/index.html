Check Before You Publish
  • The customer has approved the story, every figure and the quote in writing.
  • Every figure comes from the customer or a page you can cite; page claims stay labelled as the vendor's own claims.
  • Nothing in the recognition or company-wide claims is shown as this customer's result.
Reading the answer

This story supports a draft account of “45% of tickets resolved without a human in 60 days; first response time down from 6 hours to 2 minutes”. Source verification and written permission for the exact wording remain separate checks. A single pilot is not proof of general or causal improvement. The conclusion needs review if the original records alter the baseline, measurement period or sample, or the customer declines the proposed wording or use.

Decisions by role

Proposed owners. Confirm the named person before acting. Supplied statements below are inputs to check, not established findings.

CMO: compare “A ticket backlog growing faster than the support team could hire” and “45% of tickets resolved without a human in 60 days; first response time down from 6 hours to 2 minutes” with the original records; retain sample and period in the final story before requesting approval.

CRO: ask the account owner whether “AI agents that resolve refund and order-status tickets inside the help desk, with human approval for refunds” explains the buyer's next decision; do not promise another account the same outcome.

Demand generation head: test a reader action to measure the starting point for “an AI support agent for help desks, sold as an annual subscription”; check the destination and permission before distributing the story.

How to read the answer

This draft structures what you entered into The Story (draft), The Challenge, The Solution, The Results, In Their Words, Where This Goes Next, Sector Notes, and Distribution Formats without inventing customer quotes or figures. Wherever a baseline, measurement period, or direct quote was left out, the output inserts a question to ask the customer rather than making up a number. Treat the entire output as an internal working draft until you fill the gaps and get written customer sign-off under Check Before You Publish.

What to do next

  • Founder: Send the questions marked in The Challenge, The Results, and In Their Words to your customer contact and ask for written permission to publish their name and numbers.
  • CEO: Check Check Before You Publish to confirm that every figure has a baseline, a measurement period, and written customer approval before using the story in investor or board decks.
  • CMO: Once the customer approves the text, hand the Distribution Formats (One-Line Testimonial, Social Media Version, and Email Snippet) to your content team and update the proof strip on your website.
  • CRO: Give the approved story and the Sector Notes proof shape to account executives to use in deals facing the same buyer role and industry.
  • Demand generation head: Put the approved Email Snippet and Social Media Version into your nurture sequences and retargeting campaigns for that sector.

Why come back

Run the tool in Discovery mode before your next customer interview to get the exact questions to ask, then return in Full mode once you have the customer's transcript, before-and-after numbers, and approved quote. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

About Shashwat

Who is the Case Study Generator tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished copy in their brand voice: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders wherever your inputs did not supply a fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools do not look anything up. They work from the story and facts you give, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How does the Case Study Generator tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Case Study Generator.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
