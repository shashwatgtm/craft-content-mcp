Request Email

Subject: Quick favor: share your experience with the product?

Hello,

I hope this finds you well!

What I have seen at a food delivery company:

  • Resolved 45% of support tickets without a human in 60 days.

Would you be willing to share a brief quote about your experience? Just 2-3 sentences about what the product has meant for your team.

Here's what it involves:

  • Time: 5 minutes to write (or I can draft it for you)
  • Approval: You'll see and approve anything before it's used

I can even draft something based on what you've shared, and you just edit/approve. Would that work?

Thanks for considering,
[Your name]

Notes for you, not part of the email: the time in the email (5 minutes to write, 15-20 minutes for a call) is an example, so change it to what you will really ask for. use case was not given, so the email names no usage; add it if the customer should know where the quote will appear. Review sites suit software products; for other businesses a named reference call or a case study is the usual proof.

Interview Questions
Warm-Up
  1. Tell me about your role at a food delivery company.
  2. How long have you been using the product?
Before the product
  1. What was happening before you found the product?
  2. What specific challenges were you trying to solve?
  3. What was the impact of those challenges on your team/business?
  4. What had you tried before?
Decision & Rollout
  1. How did you first hear about the product?
  2. What made you choose the product over alternatives?
  3. What was the rollout like?
  4. How long did it take to see first results?
Results
  1. What specific results have you seen?
  2. Can you put numbers on that? (%, $, time saved)
  3. How has this impacted your team's day-to-day?
  4. What's the biggest win you've had with the product?
  5. You told us: "Resolved 45% of support tickets without a human in 60 days". What was the figure before, what is it now, over what period, and who measured it?
Recommendation
  1. What would you tell someone considering the product?
  2. Who would you recommend the product to?
Questions for this role (Head of Support Operations)
  1. How did your team's day change once the product took over the routine work?
  2. What did your agents think of it in the first month?
  3. Which kinds of cases do you still keep with people, and why?
Questions on Measures
  1. Which one measure did the customer's leadership watch before, and what is it now? Ask for the value before, the value after and the period.
  2. What did the work cost them in time, money or risk before the product, and what does it cost now?
  3. Which part of the result would the customer's own manager or committee stand behind in writing?
Approval and Sign-Off
  • Who at a food delivery company needs to approve a named quote or a logo (legal, communications, the customer's own manager)?
  • Is there anything about a food delivery company's use that must not be mentioned (customer data, security details, pricing)?

Sentence starters to offer the customer. The customer completes them in their own words and approves the final text; nothing below is a quote until they do.

Process Checklist
Before Request
  • [ ] Confirm customer satisfaction (NPS/CSAT check)
  • [ ] Review account health and relationship history
  • [ ] Identify specific success metrics to highlight
  • [ ] Prepare any incentive details
Request Phase
  • [ ] Send personalized request email
  • [ ] Follow up after 3 days if no response
  • [ ] Schedule at their convenience
  • [ ] Send questions in advance
Capture Phase
  • [ ] Record session (with permission)
  • [ ] Take detailed notes
  • [ ] Ask for specific numbers/outcomes
  • [ ] Request approval for quotes
Post-Capture
  • [ ] Send thank you + any promised incentive
  • [ ] Draft testimonial for their approval
  • [ ] Get written sign-off before publishing
  • [ ] Add to testimonial database
  • [ ] Track usage across channels

Who is the Testimonial Capture tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished copy in their brand voice: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders wherever your inputs did not supply a fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools do not look anything up. They work from the story and facts you give, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How do I use the Testimonial Capture tool with an AI assistant?

Add CRAFT Content once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. Here is a prompt built from the tested example:

My role is not stated. Ask me for my role first. Use CRAFT Content's Testimonial Capture tool. customer name: the customer contact; customer company: a food delivery company; success story: Resolved 45% of support tickets without a human in 60 days; testimonial type: Written quote; customer role: Head of Support Operations. If this tool is unavailable, ask me to open its web page; do not invent its output. In Gemini CLI or Antigravity, use the Helix GTM Suite skills package for related guidance, without claiming to run this connector.

How does the Testimonial Capture tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Testimonial Capture.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
