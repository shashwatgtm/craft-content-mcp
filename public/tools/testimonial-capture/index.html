Interview Questions
Warm-Up
  1. Tell me about your role at Example Food Delivery Co.
  2. How long have you been using [your product]?
Before [your product]
  1. What was happening before you found [your product]?
  2. What specific challenges were you trying to solve?
  3. What was the impact of those challenges on your team/business?
  4. What had you tried before?
Decision & Rollout
  1. How did you first hear about [your product]?
  2. What made you choose [your product] over alternatives?
  3. What was the rollout like?
  4. How long did it take to see first results?
Results
  1. What specific results have you seen?
  2. Can you put numbers on that? (%, $, time saved)
  3. How has this impacted your team's day-to-day?
  4. What's the biggest win you've had with [your product]?
Recommendation
  1. What would you tell someone considering [your product]?
  2. Who would you recommend [your product] to?
Questions for a Head of Support Operations
  1. What did a normal day look like for your team before, and what is different now?
  2. Which part of the daily work got easier first, and which part did not change?
  3. How did your frontline team react in the first weeks?

Who is Testimonial Capture not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want finished, on-brand copy: each tool returns a structured draft to edit, with placeholders where your inputs gave no fact.
  • Anyone who needs research or quotes gathered for them: the tools look nothing up; they work from the story and facts you give, and anything they add themselves is marked as an example, a suggestion or a placeholder.

How do I use Testimonial Capture from Claude?

Add CRAFT Content once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use CRAFT Content's Testimonial Capture tool. customer name: Sam Example (fictional); customer company: Example Food Delivery Co; success story: Resolved 45% of support tickets without a human in 60 days; testimonial type: Written quote; customer role: Head of Support Operations.

How does Testimonial Capture work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Testimonial Capture.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the CRAFT Content code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
